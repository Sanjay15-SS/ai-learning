# DP-0431 (ed. 04-24) — WATER DAMAGE AND SEEPAGE EXCLUSION

Policy Line: DP-3 Dwelling Fire | Edition Date: 04-24 | Effective: April 15, 2024

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the DWELLING FIRE POLICY WORDING. It does not apply to any policy rated on a homeowners line.

## Clause 1 — Applicability

This endorsement applies only to dwellings rated on policy line DP-3 and held for rental or seasonal occupancy. Where a risk is written on a homeowners line, form HO-0304 applies in place of this endorsement.

## Clause 2 — Restriction of Coverage

Coverage for the discharge, overflow, or escape of water or steam from any plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system, or from any household appliance, is deleted in its entirety unless the Water Damage Buy-Back shown in the Declarations has been purchased.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Water Damage Buy-Back Sublimit (if purchased) | $7,500 per occurrence |
| Water Damage Deductible | $5,000 per occurrence |

## Clause 3 — Exclusions

| Code | Excluded cause of loss | Applies to | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-14 | Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water | All covered property | None |
| E-15 | Water below the surface of the ground, including water exerting pressure on or seeping through a foundation, wall, floor, or basement | All covered property | None |
| E-17 | Constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam, or the presence of humidity, moisture, or condensation, that occurs over a period of 7 days or more | All covered property | No exception. This exclusion applies whether or not the discharge was sudden and accidental, and applies to a burst supply line |
| E-21 | Any escape of water occurring while the dwelling has been vacant or unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days | Building and contents | Does not apply where an approved automatic water shut-off device was installed and operational |
| E-22 | Loss caused by the failure of a supply line, hose, or fitting older than 10 years from its date of manufacture | Building and contents | None |

## Clause 4 — Conditions

4.1 Reporting. Loss must be reported within 14 days of discovery. This is a condition precedent to coverage under the Water Damage Buy-Back.

4.2 Shut-off device. Where a policy is renewed on or after October 1, 2024, an approved automatic water shut-off device is required on any dwelling with more than one bathroom. Failure to install voids the Water Damage Buy-Back.
