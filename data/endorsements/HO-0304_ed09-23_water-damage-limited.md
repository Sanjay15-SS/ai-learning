# HO-0304 (ed. 09-23) — WATER DAMAGE — LIMITED COVERAGE ENDORSEMENT

Policy Line: HO-3 Homeowners | Edition Date: 09-23 | Effective: September 1, 2023

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the HOMEOWNERS POLICY WORDING. All other terms of the policy remain unchanged except as stated below.

## Clause 1 — Schedule and Applicability

This endorsement applies only to a dwelling described in the Declarations and rated on policy line HO-3. The Limited Water Damage Sublimit shown below applies in place of, and not in addition to, the Coverage A limit for any loss to which this endorsement applies.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Limited Water Damage Sublimit | $15,000 per occurrence |
| Water Damage Deductible | $2,500 per occurrence |
| Annual aggregate | $30,000 |

## Clause 2 — Coverage Grant

We will pay for direct physical loss to covered property caused by the sudden and accidental discharge, overflow, or escape of water or steam from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system, or from a household appliance, subject to the Limited Water Damage Sublimit in Clause 1 and to the exclusions in Clause 3.

Coverage under this clause includes the reasonable cost to tear out and replace that part of a building necessary to gain access to the system or appliance from which the water escaped. We do not pay to repair or replace the system or appliance itself, except as provided in Clause 4.3.

## Clause 3 — Exclusions

We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by any of the following. Such loss is excluded regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

| Code | Excluded cause of loss | Applies to | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-14 | Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these, whether or not driven by wind | All covered property | None |
| E-15 | Water below the surface of the ground, including water which exerts pressure on or seeps or leaks through a foundation, wall, floor, paved surface, basement, door, window, or other opening | All covered property | None |
| E-16 | Water or waterborne material which backs up through sewers or drains, or which overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment | All covered property | Does not apply where the Sump Overflow Buy-Back is shown in the Declarations |
| E-17 | Constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam, or the presence or condensation of humidity, moisture, or vapour, that occurs over a period of 21 days or more, whether or not the seepage was known to any insured | All covered property, including the plumbing system itself | Does not apply to a sudden and accidental discharge as defined in HO-0788, including a burst supply line, provided the loss is reported within 30 days |
| E-18 | Freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system, or of a household appliance, while the dwelling is vacant, unoccupied, or under construction | Building and contents | Does not apply if the insured used reasonable care to maintain heat in the building, or shut off the water supply and drained the system |
| E-19 | Mould, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria, where such condition results from a cause of loss excluded under E-15 or E-17 | All covered property | Does not apply where the mould results directly from a covered sudden and accidental discharge under Clause 2, subject to a $10,000 sublimit |
| E-20 | Loss to a swimming pool, hot tub, spa, or their filtration or heating equipment, caused by water escaping from that equipment | Scheduled equipment | None |

## Clause 4 — Conditions

4.1 Reporting. The insured must report any loss under this endorsement to us within 30 days of discovery. A loss reported more than 30 days after discovery may be denied in whole or in part where the delay has prejudiced our investigation.

4.2 Mitigation. The insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage, including shutting off the water supply and arranging drying within 72 hours of discovery.

4.3 System repair. We will pay up to $2,000 toward the repair or replacement of the specific length of supply line, fitting, or appliance component that failed, where that failure caused a loss covered under Clause 2. This payment is within, and not in addition to, the Limited Water Damage Sublimit.

4.4 Inspection credit. Where a licensed plumber's inspection report dated within 12 months before the date of loss is produced, the Water Damage Deductible in Clause 1 is reduced to $1,000.

## Clause 5 — Non-Renewal Trigger

Two or more paid water damage claims under this endorsement within any rolling 36-month period permit us to non-renew the policy at its next anniversary, subject to 45 days' written notice.
