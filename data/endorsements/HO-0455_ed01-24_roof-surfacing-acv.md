# HO-0455 (ed. 01-24) — ROOF SURFACING — ACTUAL CASH VALUE LOSS SETTLEMENT

Policy Line: HO-3 Homeowners | Edition Date: 01-24 | Effective: January 15, 2024

This endorsement modifies loss settlement for roof surfacing under the HOMEOWNERS POLICY WORDING.

## Clause 1 — Loss Settlement Basis

Loss to roof surfacing caused by windstorm or hail is settled at actual cash value, not replacement cost. Actual cash value is determined by applying the Payment Schedule in Clause 2 to the replacement cost of the roof surfacing, less the applicable deductible.

## Clause 2 — Payment Schedule

| Roof age at date of loss | Asphalt / composition shingle | Metal, tile, or slate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 years | 100% | 100% |
| 6 to 10 years | 80% | 95% |
| 11 to 15 years | 60% | 85% |
| 16 to 20 years | 40% | 70% |
| 21 to 25 years | 25% | 55% |
| Over 25 years | 15% | 40% |

Roof age is measured from the date of manufacture where documented, otherwise from the date of installation shown on the permit record.

## Clause 3 — Exclusions

| Code | Excluded cause of loss | Applies to | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-21 | Cosmetic damage to roof surfacing that does not compromise the water-shedding function of the roof, including granule loss, dents, dings, and marring | Roof surfacing only | Does not apply where the Cosmetic Damage Buy-Back is shown in the Declarations |
| E-22 | Loss to roof surfacing caused by wear, tear, deterioration, or manufacturing defect | Roof surfacing only | None |
| E-23 | Loss to roof surfacing more than 30 years old at the date of loss | Roof surfacing only | None. Such roofs are ineligible and coverage is deleted in its entirety |
| E-24 | Loss caused by repair, alteration, or installation work performed on the roof within 90 days before the date of loss | Roof surfacing and interior water damage resulting from it | Does not apply where the work was performed by a contractor licensed in the state of the residence premises |

## Clause 4 — Conditions

4.1 Interior water damage resulting from a covered roof loss is settled on a replacement cost basis and is not subject to the Payment Schedule in Clause 2.

4.2 The insured must produce documentation of roof age. Where no documentation is produced, the roof is presumed to be 20 years old for the purposes of Clause 2.
