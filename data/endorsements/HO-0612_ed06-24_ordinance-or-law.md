# HO-0612 (ed. 06-24) — ORDINANCE OR LAW — INCREASED AMOUNT OF COVERAGE

Policy Line: HO-5 Homeowners | Edition Date: 06-24 | Effective: June 1, 2024

This endorsement increases the Ordinance or Law coverage provided under the HOMEOWNERS POLICY WORDING for policies rated on line HO-5.

## Clause 1 — Increased Limit

The Ordinance or Law limit is increased from 10% of the Coverage A limit to 25% of the Coverage A limit. This increased amount is additional insurance and does not reduce the Coverage A limit.

| Item | Base wording | As amended by HO-0612 |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinance or Law limit | 10% of Coverage A | 25% of Coverage A |
| Applies to demolition cost | Yes | Yes |
| Applies to undamaged portions | No | Yes |
| Increased cost of construction cap | $25,000 | $75,000 |

## Clause 2 — Covered Costs

2.1 The increased amount in Clause 1 covers the increased cost to repair, rebuild, or construct the dwelling where that increased cost is caused by the enforcement of an ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, or demolition, and where the enforcement is triggered by a loss covered under Coverage A.

2.2 Covered costs include the cost to demolish and clear the site of undamaged portions of the dwelling required to be demolished by the ordinance or law.

2.3 Covered costs include the cost of bringing electrical, plumbing, and mechanical systems into compliance with current code, where that work is required as a condition of the repair permit.

## Clause 3 — Exclusions

| Code | Excluded cause of loss | Applies to | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-40 | The cost to comply with any ordinance or law requiring the testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, or neutralization of pollutants, including asbestos and lead paint | All covered property | Does not apply to asbestos abatement required solely as a condition of a permit to repair covered damage, subject to a $15,000 sublimit |
| E-41 | The cost to comply with an ordinance or law that was in force and being enforced against the dwelling at the time of loss and with which the dwelling was already in violation | All covered property | None |
| E-42 | Loss in value to any covered property arising from an ordinance or law | All covered property | None |
| E-43 | The cost to comply with an ordinance or law where the loss triggering enforcement is not itself covered under Coverage A | All covered property | None |

## Clause 4 — Conditions

4.1 This endorsement applies only where the dwelling is rated on policy line HO-5. It does not apply to a dwelling rated on HO-3 or on a dwelling fire line.

4.2 The insured must produce the written permit condition or code citation relied upon. We do not pay for costs the insured elects to incur that are not required by an ordinance or law.
