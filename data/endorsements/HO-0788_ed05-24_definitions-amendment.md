# HO-0788 (ed. 05-24) — DEFINITIONS AMENDMENT ENDORSEMENT

Policy Line: HO-3 Homeowners | Edition Date: 05-24 | Effective: May 1, 2024

This endorsement amends the DEFINITIONS section of the HOMEOWNERS POLICY WORDING. Where a term defined here is used in any other endorsement attached to this policy, the definition in this endorsement controls.

## Clause 1 — Scope

The definitions in Clause 2 replace any conflicting definition in the base wording and apply to all endorsements attached to the policy, including HO-0304, HO-0455, HO-0612, and HO-2199.

## Clause 2 — Amended Definitions

2.1 Sudden and accidental means an event that is both unexpected and unintended from the standpoint of the insured, and that begins at an identifiable point in time. A discharge of water is sudden and accidental where the escape begins abruptly, regardless of how long the resulting water continues to flow before discovery, provided the insured did not know and could not reasonably have known of the escape. A discharge that begins as a slow weep, drip, or bead of moisture is not sudden and accidental, even where it later worsens abruptly.

2.2 Supply line means any pipe, tube, hose, or fitting that carries water under pressure toward a fixture or appliance, up to and including the shut-off valve serving that fixture or appliance. A drain, waste, or vent line is not a supply line.

2.3 Constant or repeated seepage or leakage means an escape of water that is continuous, or that recurs on more than one occasion, and that has persisted for the period stated in the applicable exclusion. The period runs from the date the escape began, not the date it was discovered.

2.4 Vacant means that substantially all personal property has been removed from the dwelling and no insured resides there. A dwelling under active renovation with utilities connected is not vacant.

2.5 Unoccupied means that no insured has resided in the dwelling for the stated period, whether or not personal property remains.

2.6 Business means any full-time, part-time, or occasional activity engaged in for money or other compensation, including the rental or holding for rental of any part of the residence premises.

## Clause 3 — Interaction With Exclusions

3.1 Where an exclusion in another endorsement carries an exception for a sudden and accidental discharge, that exception is read using the definition in Clause 2.1 of this endorsement.

3.2 Where an escape of water begins as a sudden and accidental discharge but is not discovered for a period exceeding the days stated in the applicable seepage exclusion, coverage is determined under Clause 2.1 and not under the seepage exclusion, provided the insured could not reasonably have known of the escape.

3.3 Nothing in this endorsement creates coverage that is otherwise excluded, nor increases any sublimit.
