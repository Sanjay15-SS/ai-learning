# HO-2199 (ed. 02-24) — HOME-SHARING AND HOME BUSINESS EXCLUSION

Policy Line: HO-3 Homeowners | Edition Date: 02-24 | Effective: February 1, 2024

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the HOMEOWNERS POLICY WORDING.

## Clause 1 — Home-Sharing Occupancy Defined

A home-sharing occupancy exists during any period in which any part of the residence premises is occupied by a person other than an insured under an arrangement made through a home-sharing network platform or any comparable short-term rental arrangement, and for the 24 hours immediately following that occupancy.

## Clause 2 — Exclusions

| Code | Excluded cause of loss | Applies to | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-30 | Any loss to covered property occurring during a home-sharing occupancy | Building and contents | Does not apply to loss by fire, lightning, or explosion |
| E-31 | Theft or mysterious disappearance of personal property during a home-sharing occupancy, or within 24 hours after it ends | Personal property only | None |
| E-32 | Bodily injury or property damage arising out of a home-sharing occupancy | Section II Liability | Does not apply where the Home-Sharing Liability Buy-Back is shown in the Declarations |
| E-33 | Any loss arising out of a business conducted on the residence premises, including the manufacture, storage, or distribution of goods for sale | Building, contents, and Section II Liability | Does not apply to an incidental office occupancy with no employees and no customer visits, where annual gross receipts do not exceed $10,000 |
| E-34 | Loss to property held for rental to others, or to furnishings provided for the use of a home-sharing guest | Personal property only | None |

## Clause 3 — Notice Requirement

3.1 The insured must notify us in writing before the first home-sharing occupancy begins. Failure to notify permits us to void coverage for any loss occurring during a home-sharing occupancy.

3.2 More than 60 days of home-sharing occupancy in any policy year renders the risk ineligible for the homeowners line, and the policy may be non-renewed at its next anniversary.

## Clause 4 — Premium

A home-sharing surcharge of 18% of the base premium applies for any policy year in which a home-sharing occupancy is declared.
